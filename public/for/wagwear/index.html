What we noticed

We looked at about 20 of your ~60 live Sport-related ads (Meta Ad Library, Nov 14). They show the boots on a mountain trail, gravel and wooded paths, a lakeside and colored graphic layouts, and the one Sport video's copy is about hot pavement. None of the ads we sampled show the boots in snow, slush, rain or on a salted city sidewalk, even though your own ad copy says WagWellies protect paws from ice and salt. The angle: the same boots, in the winter your customers walk in every day.

Accuracy check

Your photo: the reference

Every scene was compared at full size against your product photo on these points before it was used:

  • ✓Four boots, one on each paw, all Flare Orange
  • ✓Rounded clog toe with round drainage holes above the sole
  • ✓One wide orange strap with light reflective lettering
  • ✓Back tab with the small black label

7 scenes checked, 7 passed. Two scenes were regenerated: the snow scene gave the dog an orange collar that isn't part of the boots, and the first hero shot had a flat band across the top. In the salt clip an orange collar flickered in for a moment, so the Reel uses only the part after it. The tiny reflective SPORT lettering on the straps can render imperfectly in AI scenes; on paid work we retouch it by hand before anything runs. Each moving clip was checked at 7 points across the clip so the product keeps its shape. Checked by Hookset on Nov 16, 2026.
