Accuracy check

Your photo: the reference

Every scene was compared at full size against your product photo on these points before it was used:

  • ✓Three seats plus one ottoman chaise
  • ✓Khaki-olive heathered fabric
  • ✓Corner-chair ends, armless middle
  • ✓Skirt to the floor, no legs showing

7 scenes checked, 7 passed. We first tried the 5-piece closed L-shaped sectional; the module count drifted in the generated rooms, so we switched to the simpler 4-piece configuration (three seats plus an ottoman chaise) in the same Olive fabric. We then regenerated three rooms (movie night, friend crashing, holidays) whose first versions had a pleated skirt, missing corner-chair sides or a rolled arm, and redid the Sunday-morning clip to remove a curtain glitch. In the holidays room the ottoman sits on the left; it's a free-standing piece, so it can go on either side. Each moving clip was checked at 7 points across the clip so the product keeps its shape.
