Terms, refunds & privacy

The plain-words version of how working with Hookset Creative works. Questions? Email hello@hooksetcreative.com.

Who we are

Hookset Creative is an ad design studio based in San Jose, California. We make static and motion ads for e-commerce brands and agencies. We don't buy media or run ads for you.

What you're buying

  • Trial ($150, one-time): 10 static ads on one product, each in 4:5, 1:1 and 9:16, delivered within 3 business days after payment and your product link.
  • One-off batch ($500, one-time): 15 static ads, split across up to 3 products, no subscription.
  • Starter and Growth (monthly): the ads listed on our pricing section, delivered weekly and split across up to 5 products (Starter) or 10 products (Growth), with at least 4 ads per product. Founding rates apply to our first 3 brands and stay the same for as long as you stay subscribed.

Payments and cancelling

  • Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Monthly plans are billed at the start of each month of service. You can pause or cancel anytime by emailing us; cancelling stops the next charge, and you keep everything already delivered.

Refunds

  • Trial: if none of the 10 ads are usable, email us within 7 days of delivery and we refund the full $150.
  • Batches and monthly plans: delivered ads aren't refundable, but every batch includes 2 rounds of revisions so we can get it right.
  • If we ever miss a delivery date we promised, tell us and we'll make it right, up to a refund for the missed work.

Revisions

Each batch includes 2 revision rounds. Send all changes for a round in one message and we'll turn them around within 2 business days.

Who owns the ads

  • Once a batch is paid for, the final ads are yours to use, edit and run anywhere, for as long as you like.
  • Free sample ads we send before you're a client are yours to run too.
  • We only show your ads in our portfolio with your written permission.

Your photos, brand and claims

  • We build ads from your official product photos, usually pulled from your own product pages, and only use them to make your ads.
  • Please only share materials you have the right to use. We only use product claims that appear on your site or that you give us, and you approve ads before running them.

How we use AI

  • We use AI tools to create scenes and layouts around your real product photos. We never invent your product, and a person checks every ad before delivery.
  • We tell you which ads contain AI-generated imagery so you can label them the way Meta, TikTok and other platforms require. Running ads within each platform's rules is your responsibility.

Privacy

  • When you fill out our form, we receive your name, email, website and what you sell, delivered to our inbox by our form provider (Web3Forms). We use it only to reply to you.
  • If you pay, Stripe processes your payment details under its own privacy policy.
  • If you share ad results with us, we use them only to plan your next ads and never share them.
  • We don't sell your information or add you to newsletters. Ask us anytime to delete what we have.

Changes

If we update these terms, we'll change the date at the top. Changes never apply to work you've already paid for.
