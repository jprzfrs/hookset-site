Questions

Do I need to send product photos?

No. We pull the official photos from your product page. If you have newer or better shots, send them; clear photos on plain backgrounds give the best results.

Will the ads look AI-made?

That's what we work hardest to avoid. The product is always your real photo, text is set by hand in templates, and every ad is checked before delivery.

Do you run the ads too?

No. We make the ads; you or your agency runs them. If you share results, we use them to plan the next batch.

Do I have to share my ad results?

No. It's optional. Sharing view-only access or a monthly export unlocks the results review and lets us double down on your winners. Without it you still get every ad on schedule, and we plan from your brief and the ads you already run.

Do you make video ads?

Yes: motion ads, 6–8 seconds in 9:16, built from your real product photos with animated text and movement. Live-action and AI video clips are coming soon. We'll only add them once they meet the same standard as our statics.

Can you make ads in Spanish?

Yes. Any ad can be in English, Spanish, or both, written and checked by our founder, who is fluent in both. Tell us which audience you're reaching (for example US Hispanic, Mexico or Spain) so the wording fits.

Who owns the ads?

You do. Once a batch is paid for, you can use, edit and run those ads anywhere, for as long as you like. Free sample ads we send you are yours to run too.

Which brands do you work with?

Home goods, kitchen, gadgets and pet accessories. We don't take supplements, weight loss, CBD, alcohol or finance.

Do we need a call?

No. Everything runs over email or Slack. If you'd like a quick voice call first, we can set one up.
